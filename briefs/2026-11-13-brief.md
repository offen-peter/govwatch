## Cross-cutting threads

**Election results reshape both the commission and the school board.** Democrats won two county commissioner seats, per a Transylvania Times article citing newly elected and sitting officials. Commissioner-elect Carson said "we've got to get on the landfill posthaste," calling it "probably the single largest cost." Commissioner O'Neill said the results are "a strong message from the voters" and warned of "additional financial pressures" ahead. On the school board, Lanier and Kelley won seats, with board member Owen saying the board "is going to face very tough decisions ahead." Both incoming bodies take their seats before major items already in motion: the county's landfill costs and the school system's bond-funded renovation plans, including a second $18 million bond sale planned for 2028.
