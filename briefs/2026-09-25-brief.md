### September 21, 2026, regular meeting

#### Decided
- **Stop sign at Thomas St and W Main St.** Council amended the City Traffic Schedule to add a stop sign, recorded as unanimous in the agenda packet; no video or minutes were retrieved to confirm the vote.
- **FOG program UDO amendment.** Council adopted amendments to UDO Chapter 17 governing the Fats, Oils and Grease program, recorded as unanimous in the agenda packet; unconfirmed by minutes or recording.
- **Ecusta Trail Advisory Board appointment.** Council appointed Joseph Moore as an at large member, recorded as unanimous in the agenda packet; unconfirmed by minutes or recording.

#### Heard, not decided
- **UDO building height amendments.** A public hearing was held on proposed changes to UDO Chapters 2, 6, and 19 governing building height; the packet states council will act at the next regular meeting.
- **Removal of off-street parking minimums.** A public hearing was held on proposed changes to UDO Chapters 2, 8, 10, and 17 removing off-street parking minimum requirements, ahead of a state law taking effect January 1, 2027 that would prohibit such minimums; council did not vote at this meeting per the packet.
- **Silversteen Picnic Pavilion and Bathroom final design.** Unfinished business item discussed; the packet notes the pavilion design funding from a private donation has been exhausted, with council still to decide on design approval.
- **City trail naming conventions.** Council was to consider clarifying naming conventions for city trails, following a Parks, Trails & Recreation Committee recommendation; no decision recorded in the packet.
- **Sewer system SRF loan applications.** Resolutions authorizing applications for State Revolving Fund loan or grant assistance for the Azalea/Rhododendron sewer extension and for Capital Improvements Program Priority #1 sewer system improvements were on the agenda for consideration; the packet does not record a vote.
- **Cashiers Valley Road sewer extension and Rosenwald Fund surplus.** Staff were to bring back follow-up options for funding the sewer extension and for a menu of uses for the Rosenwald Fund (Fund 82) surplus; not yet decided.
- **Economic development closed session.** Council held a closed session under NCGS 143-318.11(a)(4); no outcome is reported in the packet.

#### Public comment
No recording was retrieved for this meeting, so there is no direct transcript of September 21 public comment. The agenda packet does recount public comment from the prior, September 8, 2026 meeting, when 11 community members spoke on automated license plate reader (ALPR) technology, constitutional rights, Rosenwald Fund allocation, stormwater, and parking, and states the police chief withdrew the ALPR request afterward. As recounted in that packet:
- Police Chief Wentzell: "Building trust within the community is more important to me than winning the approval of technology."
- Ashley Hart: "We the people are where it begins and we're also where it ends."
- Councilman Baker: "We need to close this chapter out, we need to do what's right, and I'm not sure what that is right now."
