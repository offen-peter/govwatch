## Brevard City Council

### September 8, 2026, regular meeting

Details below come from the September 21 agenda packet, which recaps this earlier session. No minutes or video were retrieved to independently confirm vote counts, so tallies described as unanimous are per the packet only.

#### Decided
- **Thomas St/W Main St stop sign.** Council amended the city traffic schedule to add a stop sign, recorded as unanimous per the packet, unconfirmed by minutes or video.
- **FOG program ordinance.** Council adopted amendments to UDO Chapter 17 establishing a Fats, Oils and Grease wastewater program, recorded as unanimous per the packet.
- **Ecusta Trail Advisory Board appointment.** Council appointed Joseph Moore as an at-large member, recorded as unanimous per the packet.

#### Heard, not decided
- **Automated License Plate Readers.** Item M-3 was pulled from the agenda at Police Chief Wentzell's request following a September 1 public input session with high turnout, and items L-2 and M-1 were combined on the floor.

#### Public comment
Eleven community members spoke, per the packet's account of the meeting, addressing ALPR technology, constitutional rights, Rosenwald Fund allocation, stormwater issues in Rosenwald, and parking concerns. Individual speaker-by-speaker detail beyond the topics listed was not in the record.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- Police Chief Wentzell said, per the packet, "Building trust within the community is more important to me than winning the approval of technology," in withdrawing the ALPR request.
- Resident Ashley Hart said, per the packet, "We the people are where it begins and we're also where it ends."
- Councilman Baker said, per the packet, "We need to close this chapter out, we need to do what's right, and I'm not sure what that is right now," regarding the Rosenwald Fund discussion, leaving that allocation unresolved.

### September 21, 2026, regular meeting

No video was retrieved for this meeting, so the account below rests on the agenda and packet only, with no confirmation of how any vote actually went.

#### Heard, not decided
- **UDO building height amendments.** Council held a public hearing on proposed changes to Chapters 2, 6, and 19; per the packet, action is expected at the next regular meeting rather than that night.
- **Off-street parking minimums.** Council held a public hearing on removing minimum parking requirements ahead of a state law taking effect January 1, 2027; action deferred to a future meeting per the packet.
- **Silversteen Picnic Pavilion and Bathroom.** Council discussed the final design under unfinished business; the private donation funding the design has been exhausted, and a design decision remains open.
- **Trail naming conventions.** Council considered the Parks, Trails & Recreation Committee's naming recommendation; no vote recorded in the packet.
- **Sewer SRF loan applications.** Council considered resolutions authorizing SRF loan/grant applications for the Azalea/Rhododendron sewer extension and the Capital Improvements Program Priority #1 sewer system improvements; no vote outcome recorded.
- **Economic development closed session.** Council went into closed session under NCGS 143-318.11(a)(4); no outcome is public.

#### Money
- **Police accreditation grant.** A $60,700 budget amendment (27-01) was on the agenda, part of $131,600 in total grant funding for the Police Department.
- **Property tax reports.** August collections totaled $4,770,064.36, with $2,749.53 in releases and a $97,348.25 early-payment discount.
- **Mills Avenue culvert repair.** A capital project amendment increased the budget by $350,000, tied to FEMA reimbursement.
- **Downtown parking study.** Revenue estimates for management alternatives ranged from $88,000 to $417,500 annually, drawn from a survey of 817 participants and roughly 30 stakeholder listening sessions.
- **Sewer CIP Priority #1.** Estimated project budget is $3,300,000, pending SRF loan/state funding.
- **Azalea/Rhododendron sewer extension.** Funding amount is still to be determined, pending the SRF application tied to affordable housing development.
- **Rosenwald Fund (Fund 82).** $88,969 in surplus is available; staff are to bring back a menu of options for its use.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- Whether the UDO building height and parking minimum amendments will be adopted, and when, is unresolved; the packet says only that action is expected at "the next regular meeting."
- Staff owe Council a funding plan and menu of options for the Rosenwald Fund surplus.
- No minutes or video exist yet to confirm what Council actually did with any of the above items on the night of September 21.
