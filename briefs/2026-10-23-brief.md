### October 19, 2026, regular meeting

The agenda packet lists the following as unanimous votes; approved minutes are not yet available to confirm them, and no video was retrieved for this meeting.

#### Decided
- **Veterans Day road closure.** Ordinance 2026-42 closes Main St. from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. on November 11, 2026, for the Veterans Day ceremony (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **Streambank restoration exemption.** Resolution 2026-52 grants a Mini-Brooks Act exemption for Headwaters Engineering's $22,500 streambank restoration design on Lamb's Creek and Norton Creek (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **ABC Board appointment.** Resolution 2026-54 appoints James Thomas Kilgore to a three-year term on the Brevard ABC Board, moved to Consent as part of an amended agenda (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **Building height regulations.** Ordinance 2026-43 amends UDO chapters 2, 6, and 19 on building height (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **Off-street parking minimums.** Ordinance 2026-44 removes minimum off-street parking requirements under NC Session Law 2026-39 (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **Carolina Ave. traffic calming.** Council approved up to $14,000 from Fund 82 and six speed humps, four on Carolina Ave. and one each on Washington and McMinn, with an option to study a three-hump alternative on Carolina (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **Traffic Schedule amendment process.** Council accepted the Public Safety Committee's recommendation to streamline the process, removing the public hearing requirement and moving amendments to Consent (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).
- **Downtown Circulation Study one-way pair option.** Resolution 2026-53 removes the one-way pair option on Broad and Caldwell from further consideration, following the DMPIDAC's unanimous recommendation to drop it (unconfirmed, per agenda packet).

#### Heard, not decided
- **Municipal Infrastructure Reimbursement Agreements.** A public hearing was held on adding Chapter 2-297 to the city code, tying reimbursement agreements to the capital improvement plan. Whether Council adopted the ordinance after the hearing is not confirmed in these records.
- **Burrell Ave. right-of-way.** Council discussed a request to negotiate conveyance of an unused portion of city utility and road right-of-way. The agenda frames this as a discussion item; no decision is recorded.
- **IMD Feasibility Study Grant Program.** Council discussed a resolution to apply for the grant, which would require identifying a preferred greenway project. No vote is recorded in these materials.
- **Low-Income Homeowner Assistance Program.** Council discussed approving the program and accepting a $60,000 Dogwood Health Trust grant. The pending agenda item expected action the same night, but the action is not listed among confirmed votes.
- **Fund 82 (Rosenwald Fund) surplus options.** Following the October 15 advisory board discussion, Council discussed the same menu, including Carver St. paving and utility work, the Cashiers Valley Rd. sewer extension, Silversteen Park pavilion seed money, and a tax rebate program for low-income homeowners. No preferred option is recorded as chosen.

#### Public comment
- **Adam Perkins, Heart of Brevard.** Speaking at the October 5 meeting on the Downtown Circulation Study, as referenced in this packet's summary: "Take the one-way option off the table. Let's focus our energy on solutions that improve safety and mobility while protecting economic vitality." Per the agenda packet's account of that earlier meeting, not this one.
- **Dee Dee Perkins, D.D. Bullwinkel's Outdoors.** Also speaking October 5 per the packet summary: "One-way pairs put all of that at risk. Remove the one-way pairs from the options under consideration."
- The packet records more than 15 speakers opposed to the one-way pair option at the October 5 meeting but does not name them individually.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- Final disposition of the Chapter 2-297 ordinance, the Burrell Ave. ROW request, the IMD grant resolution, and the Low-Income Homeowner Assistance Program approval are not confirmed in any record available for this brief.
- Which Fund 82 surplus option Council intends to pursue remains open.

#### Money
- **Low-Income Homeowner Assistance Program.** $60,000 from Dogwood Health Trust, with Priority 1 awards of $750 to $1,500 per applicant.
- **Carver St.** $8,500 for paving, $25,000 for utility pole removal tied to Comporium relocation, $60,000 for sidewalk and street design, all from Fund 82.
- **Cashiers Valley Rd. sewer extension.** $25,000 to $74,969 from Fund 82 for affordable housing-related sewer work.
- **Silversteen Park pavilion.** Up to $74,969 in seed money from Fund 82.
- **Tax rebate program.** $30,000 combined from Fund 82 and Dogwood Health Trust for homeowners affected by the 2025 revaluation.
- **IMD grant local match.** $12,000 to $16,000, a 20 percent match requirement.
- **City FY27 budget.** $7.8 million in property tax revenue and $2.7 million in sales tax revenue, cited in the packet.
- **County tourism impact.** $188 million, cited in the packet as a 2025 figure for Transylvania County.
