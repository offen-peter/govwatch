## Brevard City Council

### September 8, 2026, regular meeting

#### Decided

**Presbyterian Church lease and development partner selection.** Council authorized the city manager to sign a lease extension for the Presbyterian Church property to June 30, 2030, and voted 4-1 (Holder opposed) to authorize a non-competitive process for selecting a development partner, per the agenda packet. The vote count comes from the packet record, not the unavailable recording.

**Mills Avenue culvert repair.** Council approved a $350,000 capital project amendment, which includes $254,838.80 in FEMA reimbursement for change orders.

**FOG program amendments.** Council approved amendments to the Unified Development Ordinance and Code of Ordinances governing fats, oils and grease, following a public hearing held August 17 and Planning Board review on June 23.

**Ecusta Trail Advisory Board appointment.** Council unanimously appointed Joseph M. Moore as the at-large member.

**Routine business.** Council unanimously approved minutes from two prior meetings, four proclamations, road closures for the Plein Air Festival and Halloweenfest (Ordinance No. 2026-37), reaffirmed financial depositories (Resolution No. 2026-42), adopted a Remote Participation Policy for appointed boards (Resolution No. 2026-43), and renamed the Downtown Master Plan Committee (Resolution No. 2026-44).

#### Heard, not decided

**Stop sign at Thomas and Main.** A public hearing was held on the proposed traffic schedule amendment; staff recommendation left open whether council would act that night or delay to a future meeting.

**Cashiers Valley Road sewer extension funding plan.** Staff presented a funding plan drawing from three sources, engineering and design at $11,400, a $77,000 city contribution, $29,622 from Fund 35, $25,000 from Fund 82, and $33,778 from the water/sewer maintenance budget, but this was tied to the still-open Fund 82 discussion rather than a final vote.

**Surplus Fund 82 (Rosenwald Fund) options.** Council reviewed a menu of options for the roughly $88,969 balance, including a $35,000 Carver Street sidewalk design, $8,000 to $14,000 for Carolina Avenue traffic calming, and a $30,000 tax rebate program, without selecting among them.

**Automated License Plate Readers.** Council discussed a $398,299 five-year equipment contract following a September 1 town hall (about 54 attendees, 18 speakers), with no vote taken on whether to approve, amend, table or reject the proposal.

**Roadless Area Conservation Rule resolution.** Listed as pending council consideration with no recorded outcome in this packet.

#### Public comment

Speakers on the Presbyterian Church affordable housing item raised stormwater concerns, property value impacts, and Bracken Trail access issues. Butch Stiebing said, per the meeting packet's quoted remarks, "The soil has been designated as unfit. Very few ways to make it fit." Carol Hamann said her son, a biologist, told her to "do anything to keep them from developing that property."

On ALPRs, speakers raised surveillance concerns, privacy issues, corporate involvement, and whether the technology suits a small town. Doug Powell said, "I prefer dangerous freedom over peaceful slavery," attributing the line to Thomas Jefferson.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments

Staff committed to presenting additional details on ALPR safeguards at a future meeting. The city plans a follow-up town hall in Pisgah Forest in October. The stop sign decision timeline remains unresolved between this meeting and a future one.

#### Money

See amounts above: $350,000 culvert repair amendment, $254,838.80 FEMA reimbursement, $166,400 in combined Cashiers Valley sewer funding sources, $88,969 Fund 82 balance under discussion, and $398,299 for the ALPR contract.

## Transylvania County Board of Education

### September 8, 2026, candidate forum (press report)

#### Public comment

This was a candidate forum, not a board meeting, moderated by Angela Owen and reported by the Transylvania Times. Candidate Lanier said, "We need to focus on increasing revenue, not cutting."
