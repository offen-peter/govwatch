## Brevard City Council

### August 17, 2026, regular meeting
These minutes were only retrieved this period, 60 days after the meeting, and contain the first confirmed record of several decisions.

#### Decided
- **Consent items.** Council unanimously approved the August 3 minutes, the agenda, Ordinance 2026-37 authorizing road closures for the Plein Air Festival and HallowenFest, and Resolution 2026-42 reaffirming official financial depositories.
- **Presbyterian Church lease.** Council unanimously authorized the city manager to sign a lease extension on the Presbyterian Church property through June 2030.
- **Development partner selection.** Council voted 4 to 1, with Holder opposed, to authorize a non-competitive process for selecting a development partner for the church site.
- **Remote participation policy.** Resolution 2026-43 was adopted unanimously, allowing appointed board and committee members to participate remotely with 24 hours notice, with roll call votes required and quasi-judicial votes restricted.
- **Committee renaming.** Resolution 2026-44 unanimously renamed the Downtown Master Plan Committee.

#### Money
- **Presbyterian Church site.** The city has already spent $11,000 on a geotechnical study and estimates a further $10,000 to $15,000 for a possible third-party review of the Greenway Residential proposal.
- **FOG program penalties.** Civil penalty tiers for the Fats, Oils and Grease program range from up to $500 at the lowest tier to $10,000 to $25,000 at the top two tiers.

#### Public comment
- **Butch Stiebing** said the Presbyterian Church site proposal is "now a planned high-rise apartment complex."
- **Carol Hamann** raised concerns about the greenspace, animals and vegetation on the site.
- **Lauren Wise** spoke in favor of the housing proposal, saying everyone is welcome in Brevard.
- **Ken Hynes** asked about the cost impact of the FOG program on business owners during the public hearing on that item.
- **Doug Powell** raised Fourth Amendment concerns about surveillance cameras during discussion of automated license plate readers.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- DFI's assessment of the Greenway Residential proposal's financials and track record was expected to take about one month from August 17.
- A public town hall on automated license plate readers was set for September 1 at MCJCCC, with further informational sessions promised around the city, including one planned for the Pisgah Forest area in October.

#### Money

- **Available funds.** Fund 83 (Downtown Master Plan Fund) held $35,000 available; the Parks and Recreation Fund held $0 available, per the packet.

## Transylvania County Board of Commissioners

The board held no meeting during this reporting period. The September 14 and October 12 regular meetings were both cancelled, per county notices; the next regular meeting is scheduled for October 26, 2026. Public hearings held September 28 on a resolution to pursue installment financing for a new courthouse and on an application under the House and Community Development Act of 1974 both fall before this reporting period, and no minutes have been retrieved to confirm how either item was decided. A Joint School Bond Project Committee meeting was held September 29 to receive updates on bond-funded project status, also outside this window and also without a retrieved record of what was reported.
