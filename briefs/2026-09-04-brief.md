## Transylvania County Board of Commissioners

### August 24, 2026, regular meeting
An agenda was retrieved along with a Vimeo transcript. No minutes exist yet for this meeting; all figures, names, and vote counts below come from the recording's auto-generated captions and are unconfirmed.

#### Decided
- **Consent agenda**, including the Community Transportation Program application, proclamations, and policy updates, passed by unanimous voice vote per the recording.
- **Comprehensive Transportation Plan** was adopted with the Wilson Road extension substituted for the Railroad Avenue extension, unanimous per the recording.
- **Conestee Fire and Rescue contract amendment**, moving to a monthly reimbursement model with new fiscal controls for a minimum of 18 months, was approved unanimously per the recording.
- **FY26 fourth-quarter budget amendments** were approved and the Q4 financial summary was received, per the recording.
- **Tyler Technologies/Open Finance budget amendment** of $29,600 ($8,000 fund conversion, $21,600 implementation) was approved unanimously per the recording.
- **Public safety towers.** Commissioners accepted USDA Rural Development and Bureau of Justice Assistance grants totaling $2.3 million (verify) and allocated an additional $397,860 (verify) from assigned fund balance for the remaining construction cost, per the recording.
- Several board and committee appointments were approved unanimously per the recording, including Lisa Sheffield, Amber Webb, and Shane Hooper to the Personnel Board, and Sierra Lawing as review officer.

#### Heard, not decided
- **FLOCK/ALPR surveillance.** No vote was taken. Commissioner Chapman said he wants a sheriff's briefing on actual usage before forming a view; Commissioner McKelvey said he would vote no on any future FLOCK measure to protect civil liberties, per the recording.
- **Data centers.** A public commenter asked the board to issue a written position after interpreting a past statement by Commissioner Dalton as board opposition; Commissioner Chapman said he sees potential in smaller data centers for the tax base, and no board position was adopted, per the recording.
- **SNAP cost shift.** Staff estimated a combined $381,381 (verify) annual local impact from federal SNAP changes, equivalent to roughly 0.31 cents on the tax rate (verify), with no budget action taken yet, per the recording.
- **Highway 64 widening low SPOT score.** NCDOT's Daniel Sellers and Land of Sky RPO's Vicky Eastland were asked to return with a presentation on why the Israel Road to Brevard segment scored poorly, per the recording.
- **Brevard High School pump station**, with a roughly $1 million funding shortfall, was discussed with no resolution, per the recording.

#### Public comment
- **Samantha Jones** said her son was one of two boys who died two years ago in an incident where an alcohol investigation but no comprehensive investigation followed, and she asked commissioners for help pursuing justice, per the recording.
- **Ashley Hart** opposed FLOCK license-plate cameras, said the sheriff's office shares data with at least 15 other agencies with no public transparency portal, and asked for detailed policy information, per the recording.
- **Robert Jaworski (verify)** described ALPR systems as broader tracking tools, cited misidentification cases elsewhere, and quoted Asheville's mayor calling to end that city's FLOCK contract, per the recording.
- **Doug Powell** spoke on Constitution Week and the historical origins of the Fourth Amendment, framing surveillance as a liberty concern, per the recording.
- **Karen Gleason** asked the full board to issue a written statement on data centers after hearing Commissioner Dalton oppose them in an earlier meeting, per the recording.
- **Ashley Hart**, in a second comment period, urged commissioners to act on Samantha Jones's request rather than dismiss it, per the recording.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- Finance Director Megan O'Neill committed to reviewing Conestee Fire and Rescue's new policies before the contract amendment is executed and to meeting with them September 17, per the recording.
- Communications Director Cameron Sexton committed to reporting back once public safety tower sites are secured and permitted, and to exploring HAM/GMRS capability on the new towers, per the recording.
- School Bond Project Manager Beecher Allison committed to presenting the Rosman-area schools construction sequence at the next meeting, September 28, per the recording.
- Staff committed to following up with the state auditor's office by September 1 on a possibly extended deadline for a Hurricane Helene funding documentation request, per the recording.
- Unresolved per the recording: whether the sheriff's office will brief commissioners on FLOCK usage and policy, and what specific locations are planned for the two new public safety towers.

#### Money
- FY26 Q4 general fund revenue was reported at $76.6 million (verify), with education funding to schools and Blue Ridge Community College at $16.5 million (verify), per the recording.
- Solid waste enterprise fund posted $3.5 million (verify) in revenue against $2.7 million (verify) in expenditures for the quarter, per the recording.
- Public safety tower construction is now funded by $2.3 million (verify) in federal grants plus $397,860 (verify) in county assigned fund balance, per the recording.
