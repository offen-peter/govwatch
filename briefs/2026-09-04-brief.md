## Transylvania County Board of Commissioners

### August 24, 2026, regular meeting
No minutes have been approved yet for this meeting. The account below comes from the meeting recording's auto-generated captions, which garble names and figures, so votes and dollar amounts are unconfirmed unless otherwise noted.

#### Decided
**Comprehensive Transportation Plan.** Commissioners adopted the plan with a modification substituting a Wilson Road extension for the previously studied Railroad Avenue extension, per the meeting recording. A Transylvania Times report from August 31 corroborates that commissioners approved the plan.
**Conestee Fire and Rescue contract.** The board approved a contract amendment moving to a monthly reimbursement model with new fiscal controls, in effect for a minimum of 18 months, per the meeting recording.
**Public safety towers.** The board accepted USDA Rural Development and Bureau of Justice Assistance grants totaling $2.3 million and allocated $397,860 from assigned fund balance to cover remaining construction costs, per the meeting recording.
**FY26 fourth quarter budget amendments** and a **Tyler Technologies budget amendment** of roughly $29,600 for a fund conversion and Open Finance implementation were approved, per the meeting recording.
**Appointments** made per the recording: Lisa Sheffield, Amber Webb, and Shane Hooper to the Personnel Board; Kay Parker, Brenda Skeffington, Mary Short, and Deborah Yunker reappointed to the Council on Aging; Sierra Lawing named review officer.

#### Heard, not decided
**Automated license plate readers.** Commissioner Larry Chapman asked for a sheriff's office briefing on actual FLOCK camera usage before any policy decision. Commissioner Chase McKelvey said he would vote no on any FLOCK proposal that reaches the board, per the meeting recording.
**Data center policy.** Commissioners disagreed on whether the board has an official position opposing data centers, with Commissioner Larry Chapman arguing the county should stay open to smaller facilities and Commissioner Jake Dalton's earlier remarks interpreted by a resident as board opposition, per the meeting recording.
**SNAP cost impact.** Staff estimated a combined $381,381 annual local impact from federal SNAP changes ($172,640 administrative, $208,741 benefits), figures marked (verify) since they come only from the recording.
**Highway 64 widening.** NCDOT's Daniel Sellers is expected to present on why the Israel Road to Brevard segment scored low in the state's prioritization process, per the meeting recording.
**Brevard High School pump station.** A $1 million funding shortfall was discussed, with NCDOT and Land of Sky RPO staff to coordinate with Rep. Ferguson's office, per the meeting recording.

#### Public comment
**Samantha Jones** said her son and another boy were killed two years ago and that no comprehensive investigation followed, and she asked commissioners for help getting justice, per the meeting recording.
**Ashley Hart** opposed FLOCK license plate readers, said the county lacks a public transparency portal on data sharing that Henderson County has, and asked for specifics on which agencies access county data, per the meeting recording.
**Robert Jaworski (verify)** said ALPR systems function as broader tracking devices, citing wrongful-arrest cases in other states, per the meeting recording.
**Doug Powell** spoke on Constitution Week and cited historical opposition to open-ended search warrants, per the meeting recording.
**Karen Gleason** asked the board to issue a written official position on data centers in the county, per the meeting recording.
**Ashley Hart**, speaking again in the second comment period, urged commissioners to act on Samantha Jones's request rather than dismiss it, per the meeting recording.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
**FLOCK briefing.** No date has been set for a sheriff's office briefing on FLOCK camera usage and policy, per the meeting recording.
**Tower locations.** Communications Director Cameron Sexton is to report on public safety tower site locations once secured, with no fixed date beyond "once sites are secured," per the meeting recording.
**Hurricane Helene documentation.** Finance Director Megan O'Neill is to follow up with the state auditor's office by September 1 on a possibly extended deadline for compiling Helene-related funding records, currently due October 13, per the meeting recording.
**Rosman schools presentation.** School Bond Project Manager Beecher Allison is to present construction sequencing details at the September 28 meeting, per the meeting recording.

#### Money
**Consent items and grants** described above total roughly $2.7 million in new federal and county funding, figures marked (verify) since they derive only from the meeting recording.
