## Transylvania County Board of Commissioners

### October 26, 2026, regular meeting

Minutes are not yet approved for this meeting. The items below come from the meeting recording's auto-generated captions unless otherwise noted, and names, figures, and vote counts should be treated as unconfirmed.

#### Decided
- **Consent agenda.** Approved unanimously, per the recording.
- **Library Board of Trustees.** Yvonne Croca appointed, unanimous per the recording.
- **Council on Aging.** Jennifer B. Henley appointed, unanimous per the recording. A motion to nominate public commenter Tom Sweeney for the seat died for lack of a second.
- **Tourism Development Authority.** Board approved reappointments of Dion Hodgson and Norrie Lebrun, with Jessica Whitmire as chair, unanimous per the recording.
- **NCACC voting delegate.** Larry Chapman appointed voting delegate for the Legislative Goals Conference, unanimous per the recording.
- **Courthouse financing.** Board approved a resolution authorizing limited obligation bonds for courthouse construction, with an amount cited as $40 million (verify), unanimous per the recording.
- **Debt service fund.** Board approved Budget Ordinance Schedule B6 establishing a new debt service fund with a $5.5 million transfer from the general fund, unanimous per the recording.
- **Q1 budget amendments.** FY 2027 first quarter budget amendments approved, unanimous per the recording.

#### Heard, not decided
- **School funding gap.** Superintendent Dr. Lisa Fletcher presented a funding shortfall board members cited as $1,840,943 (verify), covering salary, benefits, retirement, insurance, special education, and child nutrition cost increases. No action was taken, and the item was framed as informational pending the January joint meeting.
- **Pre-K funding responsibility.** Commissioner Chappell questioned whether the county has a statutory obligation to fund pre-K versus K-12 only, given a cited $227,000 annual shortfall per classroom. County Manager Lauder said the county has K-12 responsibility and discretion, not a requirement, on pre-K.
- **Charter school enrollment impact.** Dr. Fletcher said state law requires local dollars to follow students to charter schools and that the district gets no state funding for students who move from homeschool to charter. Commissioners asked for a breakdown of where lost students went.
- **ALPR, Flock camera surveillance.** Chair McCall stated no public hearing will be held, calling the cameras a matter within the sheriff's constitutional authority rather than the board's, per the recording.
- **Allies for Outcomes contract.** DSS Director Amanda Vanderhoff reported Q1 utilization; quarterly reporting will continue.
- **Capital projects update.** Beecher Allison presented a detailed timeline for Rosman-area school bond projects and other capital work, no vote required.
- **Q1 FY27 financial summary.** Finance Director Megan O'Neal presented revenue and expenditure figures.

#### Public comment
- **Ashley Hart** cited a federal district court ruling and data on out-of-state law enforcement searches of North Carolina camera networks, and asked commissioners to ban unwarranted ALPR surveillance. Chair McCall thanked her with no substantive response.
- **Tom Sweeney** presented his qualifications for the Council on Aging seat, citing prior service on a South Carolina governor's council on aging. He was not appointed; a motion to nominate him died for lack of a second.
- **Robert Czajkowski** challenged the "what if your child was kidnapped" justification for surveillance, citing FBI abduction and accident statistics. Chair McCall thanked him with no substantive response.
- **Doug Powell** argued for legislative and constitutional self-interpretation rather than reliance on judicial review, citing Jefferson, Reagan, and Jay. Chair McCall thanked him, closing the comment period.
- **Owen Carson** praised a Blue Ridge Community College award and criticized commissioners for joking about a Raleigh legislative trip immediately after the school funding report. Commissioners Dalton and Chapman responded defensively, citing years of legislative relationship-building.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- Breakdown requested of how many of the 102 students lost in FY25-26, and the 42 lost in FY26-27, went to charter schools, private schools, or other causes, due from Dr. Fletcher before the January joint meeting.
- Identification of the 12 positions absorbed by the school system over the past two years, due from Dr. Fletcher before the January joint meeting.
- Count and cost of ESSER-funded positions still carried in the local budget, due from Dr. Fletcher before the January joint meeting.
- County Manager Lauder and Dr. Fletcher committed to scheduling the joint Board of Commissioners/Board of Education meeting for early January 2027.
- Commissioners were invited to send funding questions to County Manager Lauder over the coming weeks for compilation ahead of that meeting.
- Whether a formal letter to state representatives on courthouse funding, mentioned by Commissioner Chapman, has actually been sent is unresolved.
- Final courthouse project budget has not yet been set, expected for board consideration in November.

#### Money
- **School funding gap.** Cited as $1,840,943 (verify), combining an $857,000 local funding need and a $984,000 state shortfall, per the recording.
- **Anticipated next-year gap.** An additional $400,000 (verify) gap is anticipated from further enrollment decline.
- **Courthouse bonds.** Limited obligation bonds cited at $40 million (verify), to be repaid over 20 years.
- **Debt service fund.** $5.5 million transferred from the general fund to establish the new fund.
- **Allies for Outcomes contract.** $45,664 spent of $114,515 authorized for DSS child welfare staffing in Q1.
- **General fund Q1 revenues and expenditures.** $19.9 million in revenues against $16.7 million in expenditures, per the recording.
- **Possible tax increase.** A roughly 2 cent increase (verify) was discussed as the amount needed to cover the current gap and set a break-even FY28 baseline.
- Chair McCall, on the recording: "Taxes will not be raised for this courthouse construction."
- Dr. Lisa Fletcher, on the recording: "This success would not have been possible without the funding for our local positions."
