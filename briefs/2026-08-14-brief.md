## Brevard City Council

### August 3, 2026, regular meeting

#### Decided
**Hillview Street Culvert Replacement contract.** Council awarded a $1,108,748 construction contract to Hyatt Pipeline, LLC, unanimously, fully funded by FEMA reimbursement.

**Deavor Road rezoning.** Council unanimously rezoned two parcels on Deavor Road to the PGX district for CDP Aloe Properties LLC.

**Bracken Preserve trail naming.** Council unanimously named the fourth Bracken Preserve trail "Mountain Rush."

**Food Vendor Pilot Program.** Council unanimously adopted a food vendor pilot program with a $100 permit fee, running through December 31, 2026.

**ABC Board reappointment.** Council reappointed Lisa Conner to the ABC Board and directed staff to recruit a second member with retail or restaurant experience.

**Wastewater Treatment Plant capital amendment.** Council unanimously approved an $8,575,000 capital project amendment for the plant upgrade, funded by a state earmark under Session Law 2023-134.

**Sight Triangle and Stormwater ordinance amendments.** Council gave final adoption to UDO amendments on sight triangle regulations and on stormwater management development regulations, following the July 20 public hearings, per the meeting recording.

**Loitering, trespassing and public intoxication ordinance.** Council approved amendments to Code Chapter 42, with a floor modification to Section 42-5(C) tying loitering to obstruction of or interference with passage, per the meeting recording.

**Cashers Valley Road sewer extension funding.** Council committed $88,400 toward the sewer extension to meet a same-day HUD PRO Housing grant application deadline, with the specific funding source (water and sewer fund, Rosenwald fund, or maintenance budget) left open pending a follow-up budget amendment, per the meeting recording.

**Old courthouse letter of interest.** Council approved sending a letter of interest to the county regarding a feasibility study of the old courthouse as a possible future city hall, per the meeting recording.

**Parks, Trails and Recreation Committee appointment.** Council appointed Shanna Powell to the committee.

#### Heard, not decided
**Municipal Service District Task Force Proposal.** This item was pulled from the agenda through an agenda modification at the start of the meeting and was not discussed, per the meeting recording. Adam Perkins of Heart of Brevard raised concerns about the task force process during public comment on this item, before it was removed.

#### Public comment
**Peter Offen** urged caution against criminalizing civil offenses tied to homelessness, warned that criminal records worsen housing access, and raised privacy concerns about license plate reader cameras, per the meeting recording.

**Rodney Locks** sought clarification on the loitering ordinance's definitions, alcohol permit rules outside the social district, cruising hour restrictions, and the distinction between county and city concealed carry rules, per the meeting recording.

**Doug Powell** argued that firm enforcement of behavior laws is both necessary and compassionate, citing an example from Ventura, California, and warned Brevard's tourism economy could suffer without enforcement, per the meeting recording.

**James Beal** raised concerns that six of nine properties on Pinnacle Road (formerly Mackie Ridge Road) lost driving access because of the Bracken Trail construction, and asked council to review the eminent domain process, per the meeting recording.

**Adam Perkins**, representing Heart of Brevard, asked that the organization be included in a two-phase Municipal Service District task force process, with executive director Nicole Bentley involved in the scoping phase, per the meeting recording.

#### Unanswered questions and commitments
- City Manager Wilson Hooper committed to bringing back specific funding-source options for the $88,400 Cashers Valley sewer commitment, reportedly within two weeks, per the meeting recording (verify date).
- City Manager Hooper committed to coordinating with the county on an old courthouse feasibility study, including appraisals and cost estimates, per the meeting recording.
- Police Chief Christy Wentzell committed to improved CAD data collection, a daily watch commander report, and quarterly enforcement reporting on the loitering ordinance, per the meeting recording.
- Unanswered: what is the precise definition of "habitual loafing" for enforcement purposes, per the meeting recording.
- Unanswered: can the city legally restrict a share of Cashers Valley affordable units to Rosenwald residents, per the meeting recording.
- Unanswered: will the county proceed with old courthouse redevelopment while the city's feasibility study is underway, per the meeting recording.

## Cross-cutting threads
**Old courthouse future use.** The city's August 3 letter of interest in a feasibility study for city hall use of the old courthouse follows county-side discussion (per the July 13 and June 22 county transcripts) about lease arrangements and possible multi-tenant configurations once the new courthouse opens. Both threads converge once the Board of Commissioners resumes meeting August 24.

**Cashers Valley Road sewer extension.** The city's $88,400 commitment on August 3 was driven by a same-day federal grant deadline; a city council member noted the county had originally been expected to cover the full utility extension cost, per the meeting recording. This is a live funding question between the two bodies going into the county's return to meeting on August 24.
